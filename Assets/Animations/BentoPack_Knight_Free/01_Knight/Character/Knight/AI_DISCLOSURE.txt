Dark Fantasy Knight - AI DISCLOSURE

This asset was made WITH generative AI tools, then processed into a game asset.

AI-generated
  - Concept images (front / side / back, and detail repaints of head and hands): an OpenAI
    image model through the Codex CLI, from text prompts written by the author. Not included.
  - High-resolution 3D sculpt and its source textures: Tripo (3D generation model v3.1-20260211,
    texture model v3.5-20260815), generated from those concept images as separate body, head and
    hand parts.
  - 201 clips derive from Tripo's preset motion library (retargeted to this skeleton).
  - 101 clips (names starting with km_) were generated by NVIDIA Kimodo text-to-motion (model
    kimodo-soma-rp-v1.2, NVIDIA Open Model Agreement) from text prompts written by the author; several
    samples per prompt were generated and the best was picked by text-motion similarity and foot skating.

Authored by script (no generative model)
  - 4 clips of the main idle family (idle, idle_inplace, idle_mirror, idle_mirror_inplace) were built by the author's
    script tools/idle_author.py, not by a generative model: torso, legs and head from the character's rest pose, arms
    from the mean pose of the character's earlier idle clip, plus a scripted breathing and sway loop.

Processing (scripted in Blender; the scripts were written and run with AI coding assistants under the author's
direction; results were checked by the author and by the scripts on renders and by measurement)
  - Parts aligned and joined; game mesh rebuilt by voxel remesh + Quadriflow quad retopology (see any exception below),
    UV-unwrapped and baked to engine texture sets (Unreal and Unity channel packing).
  - Detail pass: game mesh subdivided once and projected onto the generated high-poly; head and hand textures baked at 4K where the check passed.
  - Skeleton: Tripo's auto-rig (v3.0, Unreal-style bone names) on the body; head and hand parts weighted by nearest-face transfer; rigid weights on armour plates, helper bones where the armour needs them.
  - Every clip retargeted and corrected (stance, wrist orientation, finger poses), plus in-place and
    mirrored variants; clips checked for jitter against the source motion.

Not used: motion capture of real people, Mixamo, SMPL-based data, or any third-party asset pack.
No real person's likeness is intended.
